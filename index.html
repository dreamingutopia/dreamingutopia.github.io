Few-Shot Video Action Recognition

This project investigates few-shot video action recognition, aiming to recognize action categories from only a limited number of labeled support videos.

We propose a two-level alignment approach that combines global video-label alignment with fine-grained temporal alignment between query and support frames based on OTAM. Specifically, a cross-video dynamic programming strategy is introduced to model temporal correspondences, together with a boundary recovery mechanism to better capture informative action segments.

Experimental evaluation

Experiments are conducted on HMDB51, UCF101, and Kinetics under 5-way 1-shot and 5-shot settings. The proposed method achieves the best results among the compared approaches on HMDB51 and UCF101 under both settings, as well as on Kinetics under the 5-shot setting.
